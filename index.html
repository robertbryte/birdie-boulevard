Good to know

Questions, answered.

Items marked “planned” remain drafts until final operating terms are approved.

What exactly comes with a rental?

The confirmed core is one configurable enclosure, a SkyTrak ST+ launch monitor, and a 4K projector. Delivery, setup, teardown, operator, clubs, games, and leaderboard are planned inclusions pending approval.

How much space do you need?

The enclosure is 12′ W × 9′ H × 7′ D in Standard form and 12′ W × 9′ H × 20′ D when Extended. These are enclosure dimensions—not minimum venue clearances. Send the full room dimensions so we can assess fit.

How many players can play?

Guests are expected to rotate through, but player capacity and competition formats are not yet confirmed.

What is planned for every package?

Draft inclusions are delivery, setup, teardown, an operator, clubs, course play, games, competitions, and a leaderboard. Final inclusions will be confirmed before bookings open.

Do you bring clubs?

Club provision—including right- and left-handed options—is planned but not yet confirmed.

What events are a good fit?

Potential events include private parties, company gatherings, birthdays, bachelor(ette) parties, brewery and restaurant activations, fundraisers, weddings, and community events.

Can it work indoors and outdoors?

Venue suitability depends on weather protection, power, access, room layout, swing space, and ceiling clearance. We’ll review each location before confirming a fit.

How do I reserve a date?

Reservation and deposit terms are still being finalized. For now, use the preview form to see the information we’ll request once submissions are connected.

How far will Birdie Boulevard travel?

The service area, free-travel radius, and mileage policy are pending confirmation.

How do I know it will fit?

Send the venue width, length, ceiling height, and a few room photos. We’ll assess which enclosure depth is appropriate before the event.
