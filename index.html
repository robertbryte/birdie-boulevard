Good to know

Frequently asked questions

What exactly do I get with a rental?

Our full simulator bay, a SkyTrak ST+ launch monitor, a 4K projector, an on-site operator, golf clubs, and all the games — delivered, set up, and packed up by our crew.

How much space do you need?

The enclosure measures 12′ W × 9′ H × 7′ D in the standard configuration, or 12′ W × 9′ H × 20′ D with the extension. Send us your room’s width, length, and ceiling height and we’ll confirm what fits.

How many players can play?

As many as you’d like. Groups rotate through quickly, and competitions and the live leaderboard keep everyone involved between swings.

What’s included in every package?

Delivery, setup, teardown, an on-site operator, clubs, and every game we offer. The only thing that changes between packages is how long you get to play.

Do you bring clubs?

Yes — we bring both right- and left-handed clubs. You’re welcome to bring your own, too.

What events is this great for?

Private parties, corporate events, birthdays, bachelor and bachelorette parties, breweries and restaurants, fundraisers, weddings, community events, and golf-themed watch parties.

Can it go indoors and outdoors?

Both. The bay works in backyards, banquet halls, offices, breweries, and covered outdoor spaces.

How do I reserve a date?

Request a quote and we’ll confirm availability. A deposit locks in your date — we’ll share the amount with your quote.

How far will you travel?

We serve our local area and surrounding cities. Traveling further? Ask us — we’ll include any travel costs in your quote.

How do I know it will fit in my space?

Send us your venue’s width × length × ceiling height (photos help, too), and we’ll confirm the right configuration before your event.
